Draw-Spinning of Kilometer-Long and Highly Stretchable Polymer Submicrometer Fibers

Liao, S., Bai, X., Song, J., Zhang, Q., Ren, J., Zhao, Y., Wu, H. — Advanced Science, 4(9), 1600480, 2017

View publication PDF

Abstract

A new method is developed to directly spin perfectly uniaxial fibers in an ultrafast manner. This method can tune the fibers' diameter by adjusting processing parameters such as the feeding rate of precursors. Uniaxial nylon 66 fibers prepared via this method show superior mechanical properties due to the alignment at each level of the structure.
