Functional Nanofibers for Flexible Electronics

Liao, S., Huang, Y., Wu, H. — In Smart Sensors and Systems, Springer, Chapter 12, 335–358, 2016

View publication

Abstract

In the field of flexible electronics, elastomeric substrates take the place of their rigid counterparts and enable uses such as solar cells, electronic skins, and flexible displays. This review recapitulates progress in fabricating flexible electronics based on electrospun nanofibers and discusses the open challenges facing both electrospinning and flexible electronics.
