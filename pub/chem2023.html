Influence of structural dynamics on cell uptake investigated with single-chain polymeric nanoparticles

Liao, S., Wei, L., Bouchez, A.E., Stellacci, F. — Chem, 2023

View publication

Abstract

Most nanoparticles' parameters affect their interactions with cells. To date, all the parameters studied are basically static (e.g., size, shape, ligands, and charge). This is unfortunate, because proteins have structural dynamics that most nanoparticles do not possess. Here, we study single-chain polymeric nanoparticles (SCPNs), whose structures undergo dynamic changes. We produced multiple sets of particles from identical polymer chains via a supramolecular reshuffling approach that allowed iterative reshuffling between a compact/static and a sparse/dynamic form. These particles are topological isomers because they have identical molecular formulas differing in connectivity and thus structural dynamics. We show that cell uptake discriminates between these SCPN topological isomers.
