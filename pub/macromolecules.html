Control and characterization of the compactness of single-chain nanoparticles

Liao, S., Wei, L., Abriata, L.A., Stellacci, F. — Macromolecules, 54(24), 11459–11467, 2021

View publication

Abstract

Polymers when self-cross-linked into single-chain nanoparticles bear some resemblance to folded proteins; yet proteins have clear energy landscapes that determine precisely folded structures, while single-chain polymer nanoparticles (SCNPs) have more undefined structures. Here, we construct SCNPs from poly(allylamine) with dicarboxylic acids in solvent conditions where initial chains adopt either extended or collapsed conformation, and present a systematic study using several characterization techniques to relate synthesis conditions to the compactness of the resulting nanoparticles.
