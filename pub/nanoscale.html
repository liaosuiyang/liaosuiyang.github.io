Quantification of surface composition and segregation on AuAg bimetallic nanoparticles by MALDI MS

Liao, S., Luo, Z., Metternich, J.B., Zenobi, R., Stellacci, F. — Nanoscale, 12(44), 22639–22644, 2020

View publication

Abstract

In this work we show that it is possible to use MALDI-TOF as a tool to quantify the atomic composition and to describe the phase segregation of the surface of ligand-coated, bimetallic AuAg nanoparticles. Our investigation shows that AuAg nanoparticles of various compositions exhibit core–shell heterogeneity with surface enrichment of Ag. A Monte-Carlo type simulation demonstrates that the surface Au and Ag atoms arrange in a random fashion.
